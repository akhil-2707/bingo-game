❌⭕ How to Play Katam-Kutta

  • 1. Alternating Turns: Players take turns placing their symbol (❌ or ⭕).
  • 2. 3 Active Pieces Limit: Each player can have a maximum of 3 active pieces on the board.
  • 3. Disappearing Move Mechanic: When you place your 4th piece, your OLDEST active piece automatically vanishes!
  • 4. Oldest Piece Warning Indicator: A pulsing glowing ring highlights which piece will disappear next.
  • 5. No Draws: The board never ends in a full-board draw. The game continues continuously until a 3-in-a-row is formed on the active board!
